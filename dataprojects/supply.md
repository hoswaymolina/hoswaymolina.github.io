# Supply Chain Analytics with Power BI

![logistics-services](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8b0ca7e-f38f-4b5a-b233-ebc76b53e6ca)

**I made a video share of my dashboard on Loom! Link is at the end of this page.**

In this project, I dive into Supply Chain Analytics and practice my Power BI skills. I am used to Tableau, so it was good to explore other data visualization software.

I imagined that I was an applicant for a data ananlyst role at Sword Consulting, and I was given the following case study:

**With the data supplied, we'd like you to create a business report giving an overview of our business, preferably in Power BI. You can take this in any direction you'd like, but the key stakeholders are especially interested in some of the following:**

- **Where does the company make the most money?**
- **How can we have less late deliveries?**

What ensued was the dashboard you will see me share in the video!

## The Dataset

The dataset is real-life data from DataCo Global. It can be found [here](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/8gx2fvg2k6/5).
