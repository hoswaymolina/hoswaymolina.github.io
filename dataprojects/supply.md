# Supply Chain Analytics with Power BI

In this project, I used the following case study:

**With the data supplied, we'd like you to create a business report giving an overview of our business, preferably in Power BI. You can take this in any direction you'd like, but the key stakeholders are especially interested in some of the following:**

- **Where does the company make the most money?**
- **How can we have less late deliveries?**

## The Dataset

The dataset is real-life data from DataCo Global. It can be found [here](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/8gx2fvg2k6/5).
