## The Data

The FBI's Crime Data Explorer has a wealth of crime data avaialable for the public. They make it easy to access with a [Documents and Downloads](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/#/pages/downloads) button from which one can download excel worksheets (for the smaller datasets) or zip files (for the bigger data dumps). I wanted to have the freedom to explore the data in as much or as little detail as I wished but also work with a manageable dataset size. So, I opted to download the Crime Incident-Based Data for Texas in 2024, which at the time of writing was the most recent data:
